\section{Data and Field Construction}

The merged panel uses \texttt{gvkey} as the post-link firm identifier and \texttt{fyear} as the main time identifier. Harte-Hanks contributes the IT-side fields, BoardEx contributes governance-related fields, and Compustat provides firm-year accounting measures such as \texttt{at}, \texttt{sale}, \texttt{emp}, \texttt{ppegt}, \texttt{xrd}, \texttt{ceq}, \texttt{csho}, and \texttt{prcc\_f}.

At this stage, the central empirical construction task is to define \texttt{[hh\_it\_investment\_proxy]} credibly and document sample loss across the merge process.
