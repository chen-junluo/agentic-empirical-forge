\section{Baseline Empirical Design}

The baseline design begins with a firm-year panel specification that relates the IT investment proxy to a first-pass firm outcome such as \texttt{[market\_value]} or \texttt{sale}. The starter control set begins with \texttt{emp}, \texttt{ppegt}, \texttt{xrd}, \texttt{sic}, and \texttt{[firm\_age]}.

The first-pass goal is directional evidence, not a final identification claim. Stronger design features should be added only after the field construction and merge audit are stable.
