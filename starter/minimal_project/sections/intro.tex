\section{Introduction}

This starter paper studies whether firm-level IT investment is associated with stronger firm resilience. The current writeup is anchored to the linked data structure documented in \texttt{data/DATA\_DESCRIPTION.md}, especially the Harte-Hanks IT fields, BoardEx governance fields, and Compustat accounting fields.

At the starter stage, the paper's goal is not to claim final causal identification. The immediate objective is to show how a project can move from documented fields to a usable panel structure, then to baseline results, then to a draft that is ready for further refinement.
