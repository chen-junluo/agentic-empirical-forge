## Cluster 1: IT business value
- Brynjolfsson, E., and Hitt, L. M. (1996). Paradox Lost? Firm-Level Evidence on the Returns to Information Systems Spending. *Management Science*.
  - Use in project: foundational motivation for why firm-level IT fields may map into economic outcomes.
- Tambe, P., and Hitt, L. M. (2012). The Productivity of Information Technology Investments: New Evidence from IT Labor Data. *Information Systems Research*.
  - Use in project: supports the logic that IT-related organizational resources can have measurable firm-level payoff.

## Cluster 2: Digital capability and complements
- Bharadwaj, A. (2000). A Resource-Based Perspective on Information Technology Capability and Firm Performance: An Empirical Investigation. *MIS Quarterly*.
  - Use in project: anchors the idea that IT capability is more than simple spending.
- Aral, S., Brynjolfsson, E., and Wu, L. (2012). Three-Way Complementarities: Performance Pay, Human Resource Analytics, and Information Technology. *Management Science*.
  - Use in project: supports complementarity logic between technology and organizational factors.
