# Cover Letter 模板

## Cover Letter

**To:** [Editor Name], [Journal Name]
**From:** [Author(s)]
**Date:** [Date]
**Re:** Submission of "[Paper Title]"

---

Dear Professor [Editor Name],

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "[Paper Title]" for consideration for publication in [Journal Name].

[研究问题与动机：为什么这个问题重要？1-2 句]

[方法与数据：简要说明研究方法和数据来源。1-2 句]

[核心发现：最重要的 1-2 个结果。2-3 句]

[贡献：理论贡献 + 实践启示。2-3 句]

We believe this manuscript is well-suited for [Journal Name] because [具体原因，引用期刊近期发表的 1-2 篇相关论文]. [如适用：对 editor 研究方向的 subtle 呼应。]

We suggest the following scholars as potential reviewers based on their expertise in [相关领域]:
- [Reviewer 1 Name] ([Affiliation]) — [1 句 justification]
- [Reviewer 2 Name] ([Affiliation]) — [1 句 justification]
- [Reviewer 3 Name] ([Affiliation]) — [1 句 justification]

[如有需要回避的 reviewer：We respectfully request that [Name] not be invited to review this manuscript due to [简要原因].]

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the submitted version.

Thank you for your consideration. We look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,

[Corresponding Author Name]
[Title, Department]
[University/Institution]
[Email]
